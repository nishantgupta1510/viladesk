Privacy Policy for Defined World City Gatekeeper (VilaDesk)

Last updated: November 2026

Defined World City Gatekeeper (VilaDesk) provides visitor gate clearance and resident notification services for residents of Defined World City, Narsinghpura, Ajmer Road, Jaipur.

We do not share, sell, or disclose your contact details or visitor records to any third parties. Data is used strictly for residential community security.

Market Gap & Reality

The Plotted Township Paradox

Why legacy high-rise apartment apps (MyGate / NoBrokerHood) fail in horizontal developments.

🏢 ➡️ 🏡

Horizontal vs Vertical Layout

High-rise apps expect 500+ dense apartments. Villa townships have low density, long avenues, and 20–100 spread-out plots where guards struggle with complex app navigation.

🌱 40-60% Vacant

The Vacant Plot Owner Reality

In plotted developments, 40%–60% are vacant investor plots. An investor living elsewhere will never download a 120MB app for land they visit twice a year.

💸 ₹3–5 Lakh Capex

Heavy Hardware & Guard Attrition

Boom barriers, RFID scanners & server racks cost ₹3–5 Lakhs upfront plus ₹50k/yr AMC. Gate guards change every 2 months and struggle with English software.

🚛 Construction Traffic

Unmonitored Construction Movement

Plotted townships face 3–5 years of active villa construction. Cement trucks, sand dumpers, and daily labor enter without verification, leading to material theft.
